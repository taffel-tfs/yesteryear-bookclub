YESTERYEAR
Caro Claire Burke

Okay. Here's the deal.

This is a cool girl book club. Not a place for vague conversations about whether we "like" the protagonist. Not a place where you prep talking points from ChatGPT.

We are going to have real thoughts. Critical thoughts. The kind of conversations you want to have when the algorithm is exhausting and you just want to sit with people who care about what things mean.

Btw, I'm patiently waiting for my copy in the mail. It's taking like 10 days to get here.
